Date:  2026-08-21

Route: PATCH /api/admin/members/[id]  ->  409 { code: "last_owner" }

WHY THE GUARD EXISTS
  Without it, the only owner of an organization could demote themselves,
  leaving nobody able to manage members, change roles, or read the audit
  trail — and nobody able to undo it, because undoing it is itself an
  owner-only action. The starter's own role route already sets the
  precedent with its `last_admin` 409.

  The check is written on the **outcome**, not on who is acting: it fires
  whenever the change would take the organization's owner count to zero.
  That covers an owner demoting themselves and an owner demoting the only
  other owner, and it does not fire when a second owner exists.
